Elkonterm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-26

Elkonterm ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

What Elkonterm Is

Elkonterm is a mobile app that displays the Elkonterm (elkonterm.uz) website inside the app. Most of your interaction happens on that website, which has its own systems for accounts, orders, and payments.

Data We Collect

  • Device and technical data (app version, device model, OS version)
  • Network and usage data required to load and operate the app
  • Any data you submit on the Elkonterm website itself (e.g. account details, orders, payment information), which is handled under Elkonterm's own terms

Why We Use Data

  • To provide core app functionality
  • To improve product quality and user experience
  • To diagnose issues and maintain service security

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only when required by law or with trusted technical providers necessary to run Elkonterm.

Data Retention

We retain data while your account is active, or as required by applicable law.

Your Rights

  • You can revoke device permissions at any time in device settings
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data

Children's Privacy

Elkonterm is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@elkonterm.uz
